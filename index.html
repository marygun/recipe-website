Banana Bread

A soft and moist banana bread that's easy to make!

Ingredients

  • 3 ripe bananas
  • 2 cups flour
  • 1/2 cup sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1/2 cup melted butter
  • 1 teaspoon baking soda

Instructions

  1. Preheat the oven to 350°F (175°C).
  2. Mash the ripe bananas in a bowl.
  3. Mix in the melted butter, sugar, and eggs.
  4. Mix in the flour and baking soda gently.
  5. Pour the batter into a greased loaf pan.
  6. Bake for 50–60 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.

Cookie Recepie

Yummy chewy choclate chip cookie

Ingredients

  • 2 ¼ cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 tsp baking soda
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened to room temperature
  • ¾ cup granulated white sugar
  • ¾ cup packed brown sugar
  • 2 large eggs
  • 2 cups semi-sweet chocolate chips

Instructions

  1. Turn oven to 375°F (190°C) and line your baking sheet.
  2. Whisk flour, baking soda, and salt in a bowl.
  3. Cream butter, sugars. Add eggs one at a time.
  4. Stir the dry mix into the wet mix, then fold in chocolate chips.
  5. Drop tablespoon-sized balls onto the sheet. Bake for 9-11 minutes.
